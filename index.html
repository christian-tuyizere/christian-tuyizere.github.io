Projects

A mobile-first AI business tool for small shops, covering sales, stock, expenses, customer debts and profit summaries, with plain-language questions about the business.

Smart Electricity Monitor

An IoT system that monitors electricity use, finds avoidable consumption and controls approved appliances, built around safety for critical loads.

Smart Parking

A computer-vision system that detects which parking spaces are free and shows availability to parking operators and property managers.

UAIT Assistant

An independent AI admissions and student-support assistant for the University of Artificial Intelligence Technology, answering in English and Korean from verified sources.

AI cybersecurity

Multilingual education guidance

An AI tool to help importers and clearing agents classify products under Harmonized System categories for Rwanda and the East African Community, as decision support.
